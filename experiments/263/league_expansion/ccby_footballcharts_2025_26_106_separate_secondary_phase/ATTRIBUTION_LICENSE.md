# Separate second-phase / playoff match-result research-only archive

Data by football-charts.com / Damir Devetak. CC BY 4.0.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22295583
Source original publisher snapshot: previously SHA-pinned 2025/26 league CSV extracts.

Modified by filtering only post-normal-season matches and mapping the
publisher's team names to the 2025/26 league-specific research team IDs;
classifying Championship / Europe / relegation / Premiership-split phases;
and quarantining 8 matches involving lower-tier teams. No final-result
source was re-downloaded. This database has NO model training approval:
original-source result facts await an independent witness, historical
pre-kickoff availability and source provenance rights proof.
